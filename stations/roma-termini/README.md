# Roma Termini

![Roma Termini entrance sign](roma-termini.svg)

Rome, Italy. The city's main railway station, on Piazza dei Cinquecento, and the interchange
between Metro lines A and B.

Visited in September 2024 with David.

Built in VectorCraft by **Claude Opus 5.5** and **GPT-6.2 Sol**.

This is the big "Roma Termini" lettering over the main entrance, with the blue wayfinding band
under it: tickets, information, Metro, shopping, left luggage, platforms 12–29 and the
Fiumicino Airport train.

Futura Bold stands in for the station's lettering and Avenir Next for the band, and the
pictograms are simplified redraws.

The editable source is [`roma-termini.vectorcraft`](roma-termini.vectorcraft).
