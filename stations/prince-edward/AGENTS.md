# Prince Edward station

- **City, country:** Hong Kong
- **Visited:** February 2025, with David and Kurt
- **System and spec:** Hong Kong MTR. Uses "Design spec: MTR: station name wall" at the end of
  this file.
- **Station:** Prince Edward (太子), in Mong Kok, Kowloon. It's an interchange between the Tsuen
  Wan line and the Kwun Tong line.
- **Sign:** not a sign panel but the station name set into the platform wall's mauve mosaic
  tiles.

## References and decisions

- The user supplied a watermarked stock photo (Shutterstock 1349269091; not in the repo) of the
  name on the wall. It was used for the layout, the text sizes, the tile pitch and the colors.
  The recreation leaves out the watermark and the uneven lighting.
- The top of the photo is washed out by light, so the tile colors were sampled from the middle
  and lower part of the wall, around the lettering. There the wall averages a mauve `#847476`.
- JPEG noise exaggerated the tile-to-tile contrast, so the sampled colors were pulled 40%
  toward their mean before being grouped into 12 shades.
- The mosaic is generated by `mosaic.py` (standalone, with the 12 shades hard-coded and a fixed
  seed) and placed into the document with `file.place`. To change the tiles, edit the script,
  regenerate the SVG, delete the "Tile mosaic" group, then place the new SVG, move it to (0, 0)
  (`file.place` drops it off-origin), and send it to the back.
- The user asked to crop out the excess wall around the name. The full photo framing is still
  the reference for the measurements.
- Songti TC (Bold) stands in for the MTR's Ming-style Chinese lettering. Helvetica Neue (Bold)
  stands in for the English.
- The lettering is centered on the wall. In the photo it sits slightly right of center, which is
  just the framing.

## Design spec: MTR: station name wall

First pass from the Prince Edward photo, at 1.348 pt per pixel. Coordinates are in pt.

- **Wall:** 276 × 144, cropped tight around the name with 4 tiles (24 pt) of mosaic on each
  side, aligned to the tile grid. The crop is the rectangle (126, 114, 276, 144) of a 528 × 378
  wall, the framing of the photo, and `mosaic.py` generates the full wall and keeps only that
  rectangle (`CROP`), so the tiles don't change. All coordinates below are on the full wall;
  subtract (126, 114) for the document. The grout background is `#5f5052`.
- **Tiles:** 5 pt squares on a 6 pt pitch, 46 × 24 in the crop, each nudged up to 0.25 pt at
  random. Each tile gets one of 12 mauve shades, from `#735e60` to `#9d8e8f`. Each shade is a
  single compound path, "Tiles_01" to "Tiles_12", inside the "Tile mosaic" group.
- **Chinese name:** 太子, in Songti TC Bold 66, `#f3f1ee`, centered at x 264, on baseline
  189.5. The ink runs about from y 139 to 200 and is 123 wide.
- **English name:** "Prince Edward", in Helvetica Neue Bold 33, the same off-white, centered at
  x 264 on baseline 233. It's about 225 wide, with a cap height of about 23.
- **Lettering shadow:** behind each line of lettering sits a copy in `#2b2022` at 35% opacity,
  offset 0.5 pt right and 1 pt down ("太子 shadow", "Prince Edward shadow"). It's hard-edged, with
  no blur, so it stays vector in the SVG. Chosen from variants (1 pt / 35%, 1.5 pt / 50%,
  2.5 pt / 60%) to give the lettering a little depth.
- Other MTR stations use different wall colors. Sample a new palette from each station's own
  photo, and keep the tile geometry and lettering from this spec.
